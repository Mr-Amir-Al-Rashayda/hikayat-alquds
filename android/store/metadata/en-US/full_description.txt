Jerusalem, told from its own records.

Hikayat AlQuds is a guide to eight of Jerusalem's historic quarters, gates and
neighbourhoods — the Muslim, Christian, Armenian and Maghariba Quarters, Bab
al-Amud, Sheikh Jarrah, Silwan and At-Tur — built only from reviewed content.
Where the records do not cover something, the app says so instead of filling
the gap.

WORKS WITH NO CONNECTION

The complete reviewed archive ships inside the app: every place, story,
historical timeline, quiz, local artisan and 34 photographs. There is nothing
to download and no account to create. A connection adds newly contributed
memories and server-side story generation; it is never needed to read.

WHAT IS INSIDE

• A reviewed story and a step-by-step walkthrough for each place
• A historical timeline, with entries resting on oral or religious tradition
  clearly marked as tradition rather than documented history
• A photograph gallery where every image carries its photographer and licence,
  and a then-and-now comparison wherever the archive holds a historical and a
  present photograph of the same subject
• A heritage guide that answers from reviewed content and tells you plainly
  when the record does not cover your question
• Any place retold for a student, a visitor, a child, a historian, or in two
  minutes
• Narration through your device's own voice, with Arabic voices ranked
  best-first, sentence-by-sentence highlighting and a real pause
• A walking tour planner: tell it your language, your time and your interests,
  and it builds a numbered route with walking times, distances, compass
  bearings and live directions for each leg
• An illustrated map of the Old City walls, the four named gates and the eight
  places, with pinch-zoom and your route drawn on it
• The archive as a web of shared themes and the memories people contributed
• A short quiz for each place, where every answer cites the section of reviewed
  content it comes from
• "My Jerusalem Story": a keepsake assembled from your own journey

SHARE A MEMORY

Anyone can contribute a Jerusalem memory. Every submission is read by an editor
before it appears, and you keep a reference code so you can check on it at any
time. If there is no connection, your memory is saved on the device and sent
later — never a receipt for something that was not sent.

ARABIC FIRST

The interface is Arabic by default, fully right-to-left, with English
available. The Arabic and English texts are separately reviewed writings, not
machine translations of one another.

YOUR PRIVACY

There are no accounts, and nothing about you leaves your device. The places you
opened, the quizzes you finished and the narrations you heard are kept on the
phone and can be erased from Settings. Your location is only read after you tap
"check my location", is compared on the device, and is never sent anywhere.

THE RULE THIS APP IS BUILT AROUND

The generator never invents a historical fact. It simplifies, rearranges and
summarises reviewed content, and nothing else. Every generated text says which
generator wrote it and what it refused to claim.
